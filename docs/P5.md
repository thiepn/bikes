# P5 — Cinematic Homepage, Product Storytelling & Transition System

## Objective

Turn Bike Atlas into a visually authored product experience without creating a disconnected marketing homepage.

The homepage and the interactive explorer now share the **same persistent Three.js scene**. The introduction changes camera, inspection mode and explosion state while the user scrolls; entering Explore simply hands control of that existing scene to the user.

## Story sequence

The cinematic introduction has five guided beats plus the final handoff:

1. **Understand the machine** — full-bike hero.
2. **Every line carries a load** — camera closes on the frame and geometry.
3. **One bike. Many systems** — the same bike moves into Systems mode.
4. **Look past the surface** — X-Ray reveals the mechanical layer.
5. **Take it apart without losing the whole** — authored explosion begins.
6. **Explore every part** — CTA hands the scene to free inspection.

No route reload and no duplicate 3D model are involved.

## Story camera

`engine/story/config.ts` stores authored camera keyframes:

- normalized story position;
- camera position;
- OrbitControls target;
- field of view.

`StoryCameraRig` interpolates between those keyframes with damping. The story scroll position is the source of truth.

This provides deterministic art direction while keeping the normal `CameraRig` untouched for component inspection.

## Visual state choreography

Story progress also drives the P4 inspection system:

```text
0–36%    Normal
36–54%   Systems
54–72%   X-Ray
72–100%  Exploded
```

Exploded depth ramps continuously during the final story section.

This reuses the inspection architecture rather than inventing homepage-only effects.

## Explorer handoff

Entering Explore:

- keeps the Canvas mounted;
- returns to Normal mode;
- reassembles the bike;
- clears transient story state;
- mounts the P3/P4 controls;
- transitions the camera back to the hero inspection pose;
- enables OrbitControls.

Returning to the introduction through the Bike Atlas brand reverses that boundary.

## Deep links

A direct inspection URL must not force a visitor through the introduction.

Examples such as:

```text
/?part=rear-derailleur
/?view=xray
/?view=exploded&explode=70
```

open directly in Explore mode.

The clean root URL starts with the cinematic introduction.

## Accessibility and reduced motion

- All story text exists as normal semantic HTML.
- The final CTA and skip action are buttons.
- The story can be skipped immediately.
- Existing `prefers-reduced-motion` CSS removes decorative transition duration.
- Deep links bypass the cinematic sequence entirely.
- The actual bicycle information remains available through the accessible component navigator in Explore mode.

## Asset caveat

The cinematic system is production-ready architecture, but its current visible bike remains the procedural calibration rig. Final art direction should be requalified once the P2B Road R1 GLB and real PBR materials are present.
