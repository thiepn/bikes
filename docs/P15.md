## Objective

Promote Urban U1 from P14's structural utility archetype into a complete third Bike Atlas content family.

P15 adds:

- full 42-component encyclopedia coverage;
- four Urban-specific interactive lessons;
- four everyday Workshop procedures;
- lesson/procedure relationships inside the Urban knowledge graph;
- full Learn/Workshop capability enablement;
- stricter Urban validation.

## Urban Learn

P15 adds four interactive lessons.

### 1. Why utility bikes ride upright

Covers:

- step-through frame architecture;
- swept cockpit;
- upright rider position;
- stable everyday handling;
- rider contact points.

The lesson explicitly treats step-through geometry as frame architecture rather than a gender category.

### 2. How an internal-gear hub drivetrain works

Covers:

```text
crankset
  ↓
single chainring
  ↓
chain
  ↓
rear sprocket
  ↓
internal gear mechanism inside rear hub
```

The lesson distinguishes:

- external chain drive;
- internal selectable ratios;
- chain protection;
- model-specific internal-hub service.

Bike Atlas does not invent universal hub lubrication, adjustment or service intervals.

### 3. How an all-weather utility bike protects rider and machine

Covers:

- front and rear fenders;
- chain guard;
- integrated lights;
- tires and brakes as separate control systems.

The lesson explicitly separates spray protection from tire grip and braking.

### 4. Cargo, parking and everyday security

Covers:

- rear-rack load path;
- kickstand stability;
- frame-lock purpose and limitations;
- bell as an awareness/communication device.

No universal rack-load value is asserted.

## Urban Workshop

P15 adds four guided Urban workflows.

### Everyday pre-ride check

Checks:

- tires / pressures;
- wheel rotation;
- braking;
- cockpit/contact points;
- rack/fenders/lights/kickstand.

### Rack and cargo setup

Checks:

- rack condition and mounts;
- actual rack/frame/bicycle/luggage load limits;
- cargo retention;
- clearance from wheels/drivetrain;
- low-speed handling after loading.

Bike Atlas tells the user to apply the **lowest applicable documented load limit** across the real system instead of inventing a generic number.

### Protected drivetrain inspection

Checks:

- chain-guard clearance;
- visible chain/sprocket condition;
- hub-shifting behavior;
- the boundary between external inspection and internal-hub service.

The procedure does not teach a generic internal-hub teardown.

### Fenders and integrated lights

Checks:

- fender/wheel clearance;
- lens cleanliness and mounting;
- front/rear light operation;
- accessory clearance around wheels and steering.

The procedure explicitly states that a functional lighting check is **not** legal-compliance certification.

## Safety / specificity boundaries

P15 deliberately avoids universal values for:

- rack payload;
- stem / cockpit torque;
- brake torque;
- tire pressure;
- internal-hub lubrication;
- internal-hub service interval;
- hub shift-under-load behavior;
- required lighting output;
- legal lighting or bell rules.

Those remain tied to the actual bicycle/component and jurisdiction.
